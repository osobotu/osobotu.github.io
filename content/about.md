I build learning systems for physical agents. My work focuses on how robots can extract useful information from incomplete observations, use prior knowledge to act, and adapt when their assumptions or policies fail.

**Research focus:** Representation and adaptation for embodied agents, with an emphasis on task-relevant perception, robot learning, and learning from interaction.

**Research interests:** Robot learning, representation learning, embodied AI, reinforcement learning, and foundation models for robotics.
